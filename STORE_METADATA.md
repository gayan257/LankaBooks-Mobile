## Subtitle

## Short description
A Sinhala reading and publishing platform for books, stories, and literature.

## Long description
LankaBooks is a community-driven reading and publishing app for Sinhala readers, writers, and publishers. Discover new books, explore exciting local stories, read favorites, upload your own manuscripts, and purchase digital books from a trusted platform.

Features:
- Browse curated and featured books
- Read high-quality Sinhala stories and novels
- Publish your own books and manuscripts
- Follow authors and save books to your library
- Buy digital books securely
- Discover new writers and genres
