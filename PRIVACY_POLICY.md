# Privacy Policy

LankaBooks is a mobile application focused on reading, publishing, and purchasing digital books.

## Information we collect
- Account information such as name and email
- Book metadata uploaded by authors
- Purchase information related to digital purchases
- Device and usage analytics for app improvement

## How we use information
- To create and manage user accounts
- To display book listings and reading content
- To process purchases
- To improve app functionality and user experience

## Third-party services
We may use Firebase for authentication, database, and storage services.

## Contact
For questions, contact: support@lankabooks.lk

## Updates
This privacy policy may be updated over time.
